Privacy Policy — The Last Light: Storm Rescue

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information The Last Light: Storm Rescue ("the game"), published by arkgamse ("we"), handles and why.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not run our own servers, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Game data stored on your device

Your progress (nights, stars, coins, upgrades, letters, story progress, skills and settings) is saved only in the game's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional "watch an ad for a reward" offers, and a full-screen ad between some nights. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device and app information, and interactions with ads. This data is processed by Google. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

We do not hold any personal data about you. Everything the game saves stays on your device, and you can delete it by uninstalling the game. For data processed by Google for advertising, use your Google Account controls or contact Google.

Changes

We will update this page if the game's data handling changes, and change the date above.
